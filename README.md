# Shnell Logistics

Shnell is a smart delivery management platform designed for couriers, logistics companies, and businesses looking to optimize their deliveries. With Shnell, you can plan, track, and manage deliveries efficiently, saving time, fuel, and boosting customer satisfaction.

🚀 **Try Shnell now and take your logistics to the next level:** [shnell.com](https://shnell.com)

---

## Key Features

- **Multi-stop route planning:** Optimize routes for multiple pickups and drop-offs with our AI-powered algorithm.  
- **Real-time tracking:** Customers can see their delivery status and driver ETA.  
- **Role-based access:** Separate dashboards for companies, admins, and couriers.  
- **Low-cost adoption:** Free plan available for basic features, premium features optional.  
- **APIs:** Fully functional API for companies, users, and company adoption integrations.
